Small print, in plain words

Privacy

What this public site keeps about you (almost nothing), and who to write to.

The short version

The public site has no logins, accounts or forms. The hosting provider processes ordinary page requests as described below.

The family room has closed. It no longer offers logins, posts or push notifications. Material from the former private room is not displayed on this public site. The closed /family/ page points to the separate games site.

What we collect

Nothing of our own: no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. The host sees ordinary requests, as described below.

If you leave a tip, the payment happens on Stripe's own page, under its privacy policy. We record only the amount, the date and Stripe's fee, never your name, email or card details.

The table no longer takes questions from readers (October 2026). None are stored.

The public site

This part is every page you can reach without an invitation: the table, the notes, the purse, this page.

What stays in your browser. One small thing, and only in your own browser: the language you picked (English or Spanish). It never leaves your phone or computer. Clear your browser's site data and it is gone. We cannot see it.

Who serves the files. The pages are plain files served by Cloudflare. When your browser asks for a page, Cloudflare sees that request, the way any host does: your internet address, the time, the file you asked for. Cloudflare's own privacy policy covers what it does with those logs.

Counting visits. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: a small script from Cloudflare reports which pages were viewed, roughly where from and on what kind of device. It sets no cookies, doesn't fingerprint your browser and doesn't follow you to other sites. We see only totals, never individual visitors.

What we do not do. No cookies. No tracking across sites. No fingerprinting. No advertising. The fonts are served from this site; the only outside script is the visit counter above. No "share" buttons that phone home. No email list. Nothing is sold.

The machine-readable feed. The sitting feed (/table/latest.json), the RSS feed and the notes for AIs (/notes/, /llms.txt) are public files like the rest. They contain what the table wrote, nothing about readers.

Taking something down

Any message or page on this site can be removed on request. Write to contact@educatedai.org with the link; a person handles it.

Who to write to

Questions about this page, your data or anything else: contact@educatedai.org. A person reads that mailbox.

The law

This site is run from Mexico by a private person, not a company.

Changes

If this page changes, the change is made in the open: the public site's files are kept on GitHub, where every change and its date can be seen.
